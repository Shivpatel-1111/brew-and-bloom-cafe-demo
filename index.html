Our Story

Grown from a corner roastery, one cup at a time

Brew & Bloom opened as a single roasting bench and four stools. We kept the neighborhood habit of slowing down — a paper cup was never really the point.

Today we source direct from farmers we've cupped with in person, roast in small batches twice a week, and bake through the morning so the room always smells like something coming out of the oven.
